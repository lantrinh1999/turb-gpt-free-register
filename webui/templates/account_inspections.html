Statistics and Inspection Rules

Only the JWT exp is parsed locally; an expired, missing or unparseable AT is treated as invalid. After a successful liveness check, the same session is reused to check the plan automatically, so the inspection does not query twice; rejected enqueues are also counted in the corresponding failure counts. Accounts deleted or already marked as deactivated before execution are counted as skipped, not as AT invalid; deactivated accounts are saved in the failure details but not counted as actual liveness check failures.
